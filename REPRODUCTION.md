# Reproduction Protocol

## Scope

The first milestone is the paper's core simulation pipeline:

1. Train the privileged point-observation PPO teacher on randomly rolled rectangular gaps.
2. Reproduce the informed-reset ablation in Fig. 7A over a one-billion-sample budget.
3. Distill the teacher into the recurrent binary-image student with on-policy DAgger.
4. Evaluate static roll angles from standard hover starts.

Real-hardware deployment, learned segmentation, moving gaps, consecutive gaps, pitched gaps,
alternate opening geometries, and classical baselines require unpublished assets or additional task
implementations and are not claimed here.

## Author Material

Zenodo record `10.5281/zenodo.18005929` contains plotting scripts, numerical plotting data, and ROS
bags. It does not contain the simulator, policy code, checkpoints, informed-reset states,
segmentation dataset, fitted actuator parameters, or deployment code. Flightmare, GCOPTER,
RapidQuadrocopterTrajectories, and PX4 were consulted as dynamics and planning references. Local
source snapshots and the raw Zenodo archive are intentionally excluded from the Git repository;
none is a runtime dependency of this implementation.

## Directly Matched Settings

- PPO uses 2048 environments, 200 rollout steps, and 100,000-step minibatches.
- Discount is `0.99`, GAE is `0.95`, actor LR is `3e-4`, critic LR is `5e-4`, and gradient norm is
  `1.5`.
- The teacher uses 32 uniformly sampled edge points and the dimensions in Fig. S4(A).
- The collider is `34 x 34 x 11 cm`; thrust is `6..20 m/s^2`; body rates are `+-6 rad/s`.
- Control runs at 60 Hz, consistent with the stated 20-step, one-third-second perturbation.
- Informed-reset probability is `0.5`.
- DAgger uses 275 environments, 135 rollout steps, minibatches of 25 environments, three epochs,
  LR `2e-4`, and gradient norm `3.0`, matching Table S3.
- The student architecture follows Fig. S4(B), including a recurrent latent state.

## Necessary Assumptions

The paper does not disclose task-space reset bounds, drag coefficients, fitted delay/averaging
parameters, PPO clip/entropy/epoch settings, the extra scalar implied by the teacher's 42-dimensional
fusion input, or the Gaussian action variance. The implementation exposes all assumed values in
`configs/` and currently uses:

- starts 2.7-4.5 m before the gap with bounded lateral and vertical offsets;
- Flightmare-compatible rigid-body dynamics and configurable linear/quadratic drag;
- delays `[3,1,1,1]`, a two-step command average, and `+-10%` response scaling;
- PPO clip `0.2`, five epochs, entropy coefficient `0.001`, and target KL `0.03`;
- realized collective thrust as the additional privileged scalar;
- initial action `log_std=-2.0`, an IR minimum of `-3.5`, and a no-IR minimum of `-3.175`.

The action cost is applied to normalized policy actions. Applying the same coefficients directly to
physical thrust and body rates suppresses aggressive roll maneuvers and does not reproduce Fig. 7A.
The main-text description of a penalty above 4 m/s is used; the supplement's literal below-limit
reward remains available through `--speed-reward-mode below_limit_reward`.

The action-variance floor is the most consequential unpublished hyperparameter found in the audit.
Without a floor, `log_std` falls to roughly `-4`, PPO repeatedly trips its KL hard limit, and actor
learning effectively freezes while observation-normalizer statistics continue to move. The trainer
therefore checks KL before an optimizer step and restores both actor and Adam state on a hard-limit
rollback.

Constant `3e-4` actor learning rate can still cause abrupt peak regression late in IR training. The
validated, seed-independent schedule switches every IR trial at 604,569,600 samples to actor LR
`1e-4` and freezes the observation normalizer. All three scheduled trials remain above 97% near 1B.
This late-stage schedule is an explicit stability assumption, not a parameter reported by the paper.

## Informed-Reset Data

The faithful teacher uses `artifacts/ir/optimized_single_gap_roll.pt`, generated by optimizing
mass-normalized thrust and body-rate sequences in the same drag-aware dynamics. The generator
rejects any trajectory that collides or fails to traverse the gap plane:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_ir_trajectory_library.py
```

A larger 7-angle, 9-offset-per-angle sensitivity library can also be regenerated with:

```bash
python3 scripts/generate_grid_ir_from_suffix.py
python3 scripts/merge_ir_trajectory_libraries.py \
  artifacts/ir/optimized_single_gap_roll_grid_anchors.pt \
  --output artifacts/ir/optimized_single_gap_roll_grid.pt
```

All 63 anchor paths and all 819 integer-angle interpolations in that library pass the exact cuboid
collision and plane-traversal checks. It is retained as a sensitivity experiment, not used for the
selected faithful checkpoint.

## Fig. 7A Protocol

The trainer records deterministic standard-start evaluation, stochastic on-policy success, and
on-policy standard-start success because the deposited data does not identify the action-selection
or reset distribution used for the figure. The closest match is
`evaluation/success_rate`: deterministic standard-start evaluation every 8.192M samples.

`scripts/run_fig7a_multigpu.sh` fixes IR model initialization to seed 274 while varying environment
and rollout RNG seeds, applies the common 604.57M stability transition, and uses fully independent
no-IR seeds. This controlled IR design isolates the large environment-seed variance. Aggregate
completed runs with:

```bash
python3 scripts/plot_fig7a_reproduction.py \
  --ir-runs \
    runs/fig7a_ir_seed274_base,runs/fig7a_ir_seed274_stable \
    runs/fig7a_ir_seed282_base,runs/fig7a_ir_seed282_stable \
    runs/fig7a_ir_seed283_base,runs/fig7a_ir_seed283_stable \
  --no-ir-runs \
    runs/fig7a_no_ir_seed287 \
    runs/fig7a_no_ir_seed288 \
    runs/fig7a_no_ir_seed289 \
  --output artifacts/training/fig7a_reproduced_final.png \
  --csv-output artifacts/training/fig7a_reproduced_final.csv
```

Checkpoint selection is based on an independent 8192-episode standard-start validation, not the
training rollout rate. This is necessary because the best seed peaked before 1B and subsequently
regressed.

## Student Protocol

Vanilla 1000-update DAgger oscillates strongly across angle subsets. The selected curriculum first
trains all angles for 300 updates, then resumes on 60-90 degrees with LR `1e-5` through update 1000.
The optimizer LR is explicitly overwritten after restoring Adam state. Update 900 is selected using
fixed-angle validation; the final update is not assumed best.

The Table S4-matching student is supervised by the earlier
`artifacts/teachers/reproduced_teacher_201m.pt`, not the final stable 1B teacher. Repeating the same
curriculum from the 806M and stable 1B teachers underperforms substantially, so the teacher and
student quantitative reproductions are not presented as one end-to-end chain.

The recurrent implementation preserves the rollout's initial hidden state during backpropagation and
resets hidden state at episode boundaries. Evaluations use 4096 episodes per fixed angle, no informed
reset, no perturbation force, and no response randomization. Both clean binary masks and randomized
masks are reported.

## Reproducibility Limits

Matching the simulation tables does not validate the paper's hardware or sim-to-real claims. Those
claims depend on the physical vehicle, calibrated actuator response, camera, segmentation model and
dataset, latency, and environmental conditions. The independent simulator should therefore be read
as a quantitative reproduction of the disclosed method under explicit replacement assumptions, not
as an exact reconstruction of the authors' private system.
